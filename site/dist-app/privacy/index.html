Privacy

You do not need an account to read anything here. An account is only for submitting a kit or keeping a private collection, and it works through GitHub.

This website

It is a static site on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub, which hosts it, keeps its own server logs.

If you sign in

Signing in happens in a separate app. It asks GitHub for your username only (the read:user permission), does not keep your GitHub access token, and stores your username, your numeric GitHub id, the dates you signed in, your saved kits and group names, and your submissions. It uses one sign-in cookie that lasts a few hours. Nothing is sold or shared, and no page shows your collection to anyone else.

You can download your data and delete your account at any time from My collection. Deleting removes your collection, waiting submissions and account record; decided submissions stay in the catalogue's history without your name. Nothing in the app shows your collection to anyone else. Maintainers see your submissions and your username next to them. The person who runs the app can technically read the database, because that is how a database works; they do not look through collections.

What exactly is kept

  • Your GitHub username and numeric id, the date you first signed in and the date you last did.
  • The kits you saved and the group names you typed (up to 300 items; up to 40 can be added an hour).
  • Your submissions: the repository link, what GitHub says about it, the checks and the maintainers' decision.
  • One sign-in cookie that lasts a few hours. No advertising or analytics cookies; nothing is sold or shared.
