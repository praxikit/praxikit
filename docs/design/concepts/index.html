Three directions for Praxikit

Each concept is a clickable prototype of three screens (home, all kits, one listing) built from the real catalogue. They are light-theme sketches, not production code.

CONCEPT A

Ledger

The receipt is the product. A ruled, typographic world: the catalogue is a ledger of what was read, when and by whom.

  • Best at making “a person read this” visible
  • Dense and scannable, very calm
  • Least playful, little imagery
HomeKitsListing
CONCEPT B

Workbench

A tool you drive from the keyboard. A three-pane app shell: sidebar, list and detail, with the receipt in the detail pane.

  • Fastest for repeat visitors and maintainers
  • Feels like a developer tool, not a website
  • Weakest first impression for a newcomer
HomeKitsListing
CONCEPT C

Shelf

Kits are objects. Colourful generated covers on a shelf; turn one over and the label on the back is the receipt.

  • Most memorable and shareable
  • Colour sorts by type at a glance
  • Risks looking like a toy store, and is the busiest
HomeKitsListing
